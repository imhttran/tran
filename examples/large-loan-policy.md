# Large Loan Servicing Policy

## Missed Payments

When a borrower misses a scheduled loan payment, the account enters a grace period. During this period the borrower may submit the required payment without the account immediately being classified as delinquent.

The grace period lasts fifteen days. The servicing system continues to track the outstanding payment during this time. The borrower may receive reminders explaining that payment is still required and providing information about available payment methods.

If the required payment is not received before the grace period expires, the account becomes delinquent. The servicing system records the delinquency and may generate additional notifications for the borrower.

A delinquent borrower may be contacted by the servicing team. The borrower may be offered an opportunity to establish a payment arrangement depending on the loan agreement and applicable servicing rules.

If payments remain unpaid for an extended period, the loan may eventually become eligible for collection activity. Before collection activity begins, additional notices may be sent to the borrower.

## Payment Processing

Payments received from borrowers are recorded by the servicing system. Each payment includes an amount, payment date, payment method, and unique payment identifier.

Payments are generally applied first to outstanding interest and then to principal. Additional amounts may be applied according to the terms of the loan agreement.

Duplicate payments should be detected using the payment identifier. A duplicate payment should not automatically be applied to the same obligation twice.
