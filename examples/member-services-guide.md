# Member Services Guide

## Grace Period Requests

A borrower who cannot meet an upcoming due date may request an extended grace period. An approved extension moves the date on which the account would otherwise become delinquent, but it is not granted automatically and does not reduce any amount owed.

## Fee Waivers

A late fee may be waived at the servicer's discretion. A waiver request must be submitted before the fee is assessed; a fee that has already been charged is not removed automatically.

## Payment Arrangements

A borrower whose account has become delinquent may agree to a payment arrangement that spreads the past-due amount across future scheduled payments. An arrangement does not change the original due dates.

## Partial Payment Handling

A partial payment is held and applied once the full scheduled amount is received. Interest continues to accrue on the unpaid balance while a partial payment is held.

## Statement Delivery

Account statements are issued monthly. A borrower may choose paper or electronic delivery, and a change to the delivery method takes effect with the next billing cycle.

## Dispute Resolution

A borrower who believes a payment was misapplied may open a dispute. The servicer reviews the payment history and responds within thirty days, and a disputed amount is not reported as delinquent while the review is open.

## Payoff Requests

A borrower may request a payoff quote, which states the amount required to satisfy the loan on a given date. A quote is valid for ten days because interest continues to accrue.

## Contact Channels

Support is available by phone and through the online portal. A borrower contacting support about an online payment should have the payment code handy.
