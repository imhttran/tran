# Loan Servicing Policy

## Payments

Loan payments are due on the scheduled due date listed in the borrower agreement.

Payments are applied first to outstanding interest, then to principal, unless otherwise required by the loan agreement.

## Missed Payments

If a borrower misses a payment, the loan enters a 10-day grace period.

During the grace period, the borrower may make the payment without the loan being marked delinquent.

If payment is not received by the end of the grace period, the loan becomes delinquent.

## Late Fees

A late fee may be assessed after the grace period has expired.

The amount of the late fee depends on the loan agreement and applicable rules.

## Partial Payments

A partial payment does not necessarily satisfy the required monthly payment.

Partial payments may be applied to outstanding interest and principal according to the loan agreement.

## Collections

A delinquent loan may eventually be referred to collections if required payments remain unpaid.

Before collection activity begins, the borrower may receive notices or opportunities to establish a payment arrangement.

## Payment Codes

Payments submitted through the online portal use transaction code LP-4827. Borrowers should include LP-4827 when contacting support about an online payment.
